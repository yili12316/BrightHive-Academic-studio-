Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

The BrightHive Academic Studio Family Portal shows academy records to authorized families. We take family and student privacy seriously and handle information only as needed to provide this service.

Information we handle

  • Name, email address, and profile image provided through Google sign-in.
  • Student profiles created by the academy and linked to a parent account.
  • Classes, schedules, homework, assessment scores, teacher comments, invoices, and academy updates.
  • Parent name, email address, and phone; student name, grade, school, dismissal time, and class preferences submitted through the enrollment forms.

Enrollment inquiries

Enrollment details are used to respond to family inquiries. Parent contact details, student names, grades, school and dismissal details, and class preferences are stored in a private Supabase table accessible only to authorized academy staff through the management dashboard. Once a verified email sender is configured, the system will also send a notification to Brighthive168@gmail.com. Email notifications are not configured yet, so applications are saved but no email is sent automatically. Do not include medical, identity-document, or other sensitive information.

Browser storage

The enrollment site uses local browser storage to remember your language preference. Advertising and analytics tracking are not enabled.

How we use information

Sign-in information verifies parent identity, displays academy records for linked students, remembers account preferences, and helps protect the service. Enrollment details are used only to contact families and understand learning needs.

Storage and access

Account identity and Family Portal records are stored in Supabase, where parents can read only student records linked to their account. Enrollment submissions are stored in a separate private table and are viewable only by authorized staff in the management dashboard; the public and unauthorized accounts cannot read them. Google OAuth secrets are not stored in website files.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Enrollment submissions are retained in a private Supabase table while needed to respond to inquiries; families may contact the academy to request access, correction, or deletion. If email notifications are enabled, a copy is also retained under the admissions inbox’s retention rules. Other records are kept according to the academy’s operational and legal obligations.

Family requests

Contact the academy to request access, correction, or deletion of account information. Academy staff verify and update official records such as student scores and invoices.
